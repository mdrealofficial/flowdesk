Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

FlowDesk ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to safeguarding your privacy. This policy describes our practices regarding user data, permissions, and security.

Core Commitment: FlowDesk is a local-first Chrome extension. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize your personal information. All your tasks and contact data stay on your personal device.

1. Zero Data Collection & Tracking

When you use FlowDesk:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, emails, addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No Browsing History: FlowDesk does not monitor, inspect, or store your browser tabs, history, or web traffic.
  • No Remote Analytics: We do not use Google Analytics, cookies, telemetry scripts, or fingerprinting.
  • No External Servers: The extension makes zero outbound network requests to any server.

2. Use of Permissions

FlowDesk requests only one minimal Chrome permission:

  • storage permission: Used exclusively to persist your tasks, stage statuses, recurrence chains, and daily streak counts in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local).

3. Data Storage & Portability

All data generated inside FlowDesk remains locally inside your browser sandbox. You can export a full JSON backup of your workspace at any time via the dashboard. You may also wipe your data at any time by clearing completed tasks or uninstalling the extension.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

Because we do not collect any user data, we cannot and do not share, sell, or disclose any user information to third parties, advertisers, or government entities.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or FlowDesk, please reach out via our Support page.
