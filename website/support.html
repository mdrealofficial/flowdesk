Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my task data stored?

All data is stored directly in your browser using Chrome's local storage API. Nothing is sent to any remote servers.

How do I back up my tasks?

Open the FlowDesk dashboard and click the Export button in the top navigation bar. It will download a complete JSON backup file.

How do recurring tasks work?

When you mark a repeating task as Done, FlowDesk automatically creates the next occurrence in your Backlog with an updated future due date.
