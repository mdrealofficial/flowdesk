# Privacy Policy for FlowDesk — Task CRM

**Last Updated:** November 14, 2026

FlowDesk ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure when you use the FlowDesk Chrome extension.

---

### 1. No Data Collection or Transmission

FlowDesk operates under a **local-first, privacy-by-design** principle:

- **We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data.**
- **No external servers:** FlowDesk does not communicate with any external backend, API, analytics service, or third-party server.
- **No tracking:** We do not track your browsing activity, history, device information, or IP address.

---

### 2. Permissions & Data Storage

FlowDesk requests only the single minimum permission required to function:

- **`storage` permission:** Used solely to persist your tasks, contacts, pipeline stages, and completion history locally on your computer via Chrome's native `chrome.storage.local` API.

All information you input (such as task titles, notes, contacts, and due dates) stays strictly on your local device.

---

### 3. Data Export, Import, and Deletion

- **Export & Backup:** You can export your full workspace at any time as a JSON file from the FlowDesk dashboard.
- **Deletion:** You can remove individual tasks or clear your entire data set at any time. If you uninstall the extension, all stored local data is removed by Chrome automatically.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services

FlowDesk does not integrate with, transmit data to, or embed any third-party analytics, advertisements, or tracking scripts.

---

### 5. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published in this repository with an updated revision date.
